## Purpose

Give the owner his working discipline on the employer machine from day one, without moving any personal system or personal data there. The valuable, portable part of the source system is the discipline — conventions, standards, session habits — which is text and travels cleanly; the infrastructure (the personal hub, message buses, personal stores) never moves.

## The installation-package model (owner design, 2026-08-03)

The committed repo is a **seed, permanently generic**. It ships two things:

1. **The corpus (the genome):** the work-usable source system conventions in modular, generic form — task convention, session discipline, engineering standards, portable skills, idea/direction notes. Never pre-specialized to any employer.
2. **The unfold procedure (`UNFOLD.md`):** the installer. On the work device, the first session runs it: **detect** (read the work docs the owner points it at — AI-tooling policy, device policy — and inspect the device: what tooling exists, what is reachable) → **resolve** the adaptation ledger (A1–A6 below) from that evidence → **generate** the adapted form that best serves him there — the applicable law subset, skill subset, tool wiring — into `local/`.

Binding properties of the unfold:

- **Output stays machine-local.** The unfolded form is work-derived content (it was produced by reading employer docs), so it lands in gitignored `local/` and never pushes back. The seed in git never specializes; each machine unfolds its own instantiation.
- **Tooling-agnostic bootstrap.** `UNFOLD.md` is plain markdown followable by ANY capable agent — or by the owner manually — because which AI tooling is sanctioned is itself a question the unfold answers. Its own first step (detect) determines what executes the rest. No dependency on any specific harness, language runtime, or feature.
- **Degraded path.** If no AI tooling is allowed on the device at all, the unfold degrades to a manual checklist and the corpus still serves as reading-form conventions. The package is useful at every policy outcome.
- **Re-runnable.** Policy or tooling changes at work → re-run the unfold; it re-resolves the ledger and regenerates `local/`. Idempotent by design.

## Settled principles (founding conversation, 2026-08-03)

1. **Zero personal corpus on the work machine.** No profile/self-model, finances, immigration, people, ledgers, or message access. The corp device is employer-managed and monitored.
2. **Know-how, never infrastructure.** What ports is the law/convention layer; personal systems stay home.
3. **One-way flow, personal → generic → work; no sync channel.** Committed content is employer-generic. Work-derived content stays machine-local (`local/`) and never pushes back. Mirrors the owner's IP rule in both directions.
4. **Scope: work-usable parts of the source system + idea/direction notes.** Nothing else.
5. **Build-before-start-date is itself an IP measure:** everything committed predates employment, authored on personal equipment — unambiguously personal work.
6. **Installation-package model:** the repo is a self-unfolding seed (section above), never a finished per-employer artifact.
7. **Operating-posture calibration (owner directive, 2026-08-06):** the seed optimizes for sustainable, reliable delivery at minimal cognitive cost, with deliberate relationship investment — not for maximal visible output. Conventions earn their place by reducing the attention a unit of delivered work costs; polish beyond the team's actual bar folds away by default; working-with-the-org practice (expectation alignment, relationship building, visibility) is first-class corpus content, not an afterthought.
8. **Boundary discipline (owner-ratified 2026-08-06; reconceived at the owner's 2026-08-17 rulings):** the running work-side installation is self-contained — it runs on the work account, work surfaces, and work budget, and holds day-to-day operations inside the employer boundary; the employment's wider meaning for the owner's life is his own affair beyond it. Two binding rules: employer-confidential content stays in employer systems (the IP rule; gray flows decided by work policy, default no) · every record the owner keeps on the machine is written at MEANING grain — his own effort-and-value accounting, never employer-confidential material — so whatever he later does with his own records is safe by construction, with no filtering or staging apparatus anywhere. When the owner brings a posture or convention update, sessions ingest what he states and never ask for more. Canonical: `corpus/boundary-protocol.md`.
9. **Time-and-presence efficiency (owner directive, 2026-08-09):** the operating posture's second target, alongside principle 7's sustainable-delivery target: deliver the expected impact at minimal total time-and-effort spend. On-site presence is calibrated to the team's REAL practice, never to assumption and never to the written policy alone — intake discovers the actual norm from BOTH the official docs AND direct conversation with teammates (which days people really attend, hours actually kept, how presence is read on the team), then sets presence at the level within that norm that fully serves delivery and relationship investment, not above it by default. In-office time is spent deliberately: the presence-dependent work (1:1s, demos, informal relationship investment) concentrates onto on-site days. Intake items: `knowledge/week1-intake.md` §10.
10. **Team-reality conformance + whole-team collaboration (owner directive, 2026-08-10):** work is not a personal project. A team or org's real conventions may be long-lived, non-standard by outside measures, non-optimal, and documented nowhere — carried in the codebase, its review history, and people's heads. Three consequences. (a) *Discovery:* principle 9's dual-source rule generalizes to ALL conventions — the real practice is discovered from the official docs AND the artifacts (code, merged PRs, review threads) AND teammates directly; where practice diverges from the written guide, the practice IS the convention. (b) *Conformance:* on any team-visible surface the team's real convention wins over the corpus's "optimal" defaults, and non-optimal is not a defect to fix unilaterally — convention change is a social act, proposed through the team's own channels after standing is earned. (c) *Collaboration scope:* the optimization target is the best work RESULT through the best human-agent collaboration, where the humans include the owner's colleagues, not just the owner — agent-assisted work is written for the teammates who review and maintain it (team idiom, reviewable sizes), the team's actual norms on AI use are discovered at intake and followed, and agent output pace calibrates to what the human review loop can absorb. Intake items: `knowledge/week1-intake.md` §11.
11. **Whole-arc project lifecycle (owner directive, 2026-08-12):** projects are the unit of career-visible work — what is worked on determines delivered impact more than how well it is executed — so the project arc is a FULL standard workflow, never a decision checklist at the accept/refuse moment: continuous sourcing of candidates → active per-candidate intelligence (docs, artifacts, and going to ask people directly — the principle-10 triple source applied to projects) → criteria-based evaluate/decide (alignment · legibility · ownership · growth · dead-end signals · cost · people; outcomes accept / shape / decline-via-priorities / chase / initiate) → communication and negotiation (refusal routed through prioritization, never a bare no; chasing before staffing; the manager as the standing channel) → an initiative lane (own proposals earn their pitch through a cheap time-boxed PoC, through the team's own channel, calibrated to standing) → daily dev under the project frame → periodic sync and presentation riding the team's own ceremonies → landing at a declared end (landed / handed off / killed — all explicit; extension is a conscious re-bet, never a silent lapse). The pipeline runs on a standing work-local portfolio board (unfold-seeded `local/state/projects.md`); selection power is earned — early tenure delivers assigned work reliably while sourcing runs in the background. Module: `corpus/project-lifecycle.md`; intake items: `knowledge/week1-intake.md` §12.
12. **Standing company intelligence (owner directive, 2026-08-12; owner-broadened same sitting twice — from direction-only to the general people/events watch, then from org-scoped to company-wide):** what the company's leadership and high-level people are doing is watched continuously, never assumed — what they formally lead, visibly push, and casually say; who is promoted and for what; who departs (exited or laid off) and for what; reorgs, funding, staffing; and the direction it all adds up to. **Scope is the whole company (owner ruling): the own org is watched most closely, but proximity weights attention — it never draws the boundary**; cross-org signals show company direction early and mark people worth binding beyond the silo. Design-criterion ruling, same sitting: the seed's systems are designed to the LONG-TERM BEST PRACTICE — smallness of footprint is an acceptable outcome, never the design objective. 13. **Health floors + the work/personal wall (owner directive 2026-08-13, amended same sitting):** health ranks above the owner's personal life, and both rank far above work — work never buys anything with health. The guard is a FLOOR binding before both delivery targets (principles 7 and 9 optimize strictly inside it): hard floors (sleep · real meals · a hard daily stop · movement/eye cadence · symptoms outrank commitments), enforced — a session that sees a floor being crossed names it at that moment; a floor is crossed only by the owner's explicit named exception; breaches are logged and repeated breaches surface in the weekly debrief. 14. **The work record — private truth, rendered public view (owner directive, 2026-08-14):** everything done is recorded, day by day, in ONE private full-grain record on the work machine (`local/state/worklog.md`, unfold-seeded) — activity across and around projects (delivered/progressed work, meetings, help given, ops, learning, candid notes), deliberately NOT the project tracker: the portfolio board holds per-project state, the record cites and never duplicates. 15. **The staged development funnel (owner directive, 2026-08-15):** within an accepted project, execution itself is a structured workflow — decisions settled by cheap evidence, never upfront commitment or debate: a whole-product design pass (through the team's design ritual where one exists; the accept-time premortem aims the earliest experiments at the riskiest assumptions) → substructure design naming components, seams, and the consequential decision points, each with named candidates (the team's incumbent always among them) → per-decision choice tournaments: small time-boxed pre-experiments whose purpose is eliminating the clearly-too-bad cheaply, survivors advancing, every outcome one-lined into the project record → the whole product built as a FEW comparable variants where surviving choices genuinely differ (same seam, same eval harness; variant count set by what the ladder ahead can actually test) → the environment ladder: local test → shared test environment (test AND monitor real telemetry) → the surviving one or two variants to production through the team's release ritual, monitored, previous configuration restorable until the ramp completes → completion DECLARED at the lifecycle's landing bar. Proportionality binds throughout: candidate/variant count and rung formality scale with the cost of being wrong plus the cost of changing later; trivial reversible choices are simply made. Progress reports and presentations are STAGE EVENTS, not afterthoughts — every transition yields a showable artifact (design readout, tournament results, variant comparison, launch readout) riding the lifecycle's sync cadence on the team's own ceremonies; the report is the record filtered for audience, never a parallel narrative. One running project record per active project: the team's doc convention first, else `local/state/projects/<name>.md`. ## The four-axis build model (owner framing, 2026-08-04)

The project's content is built along four axes — a provenance layering, each later axis refining or overwriting the earlier (legacy → imagined → observed → lived):

1. **Axis 1 — source system legacy.** The corpus distilled from the owner's personal working conventions (v0 landed 2026-08-03). The source system is LIVING: the seed re-derives from it, and refresh passes before install are expected rather than exceptional.
2. **Axis 2 — imagined role workflow.** Pre-start, public-source research on the role archetype (applied scientist on an LLM copilot/product team): the likely shape of the work and the workflows/procedures that serve it. Output: a `knowledge/` digest, corpus deltas, and an **assumptions ledger** whose entries become concrete detect questions for the unfold. Provisional by construction — every entry is a guess that axis 3 confirms or refutes at intake.
3. **Axis 3 — intake at start.** The unfold's detect phase plus the first weeks' gathering of internal docs, tooling, and environment on the work device. Work-derived, therefore machine-local (`local/`), never committed.
4. **Axis 4 — lived practice.** Long-term accumulation of real work practices and data on the work device. Machine-local forever.

Binding consequence: **the committed seed evolves only via axes 1–2** (authored on the owner's side, clean provenance). Axes 3–4 never flow back through this repo — the backflow discipline in `ideas.md` applies without exception.
