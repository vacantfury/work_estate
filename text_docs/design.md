## Purpose

Give the user a full working discipline on an employer machine from day one. The valuable, portable part of a working system is its discipline — conventions, standards, session habits — which is text and travels cleanly; the package ships that discipline as a seed and founds the working estate in place, rather than transplanting any existing system.

## The installation-package model (settled 2026-08-03)

The committed repo is a **seed, permanently generic**. It ships two things:

1. **The corpus (the genome):** the work-usable conventions in modular, generic form — task convention, session discipline, engineering standards, portable skills, idea/direction notes. Never pre-specialized to any employer.
2. **The unfold procedure (`UNFOLD.md`):** the installer. On the work device, the first session runs it: **detect** (read the work docs the user points it at — AI-tooling policy, device policy — and inspect the device: what tooling exists, what is reachable) → **resolve** the adaptation ledger (A1–A6 below) from that evidence → **found the estate** (principle 19): the work-side projects, each with its own instruction layer, skill subset, task file, and state, adapted per the ledger. The seed clone's own `local/` keeps only the ledger and the unfold log.

Binding properties of the unfold:

- **Output stays machine-local.** The unfolded form is work-derived content (it was produced by reading employer docs), so it lands machine-local — the founded estate projects plus the seed clone's gitignored `local/` — and never pushes back to the seed's remote. The seed in git never specializes; each machine unfolds its own instantiation.
- **Tooling-agnostic bootstrap.** `UNFOLD.md` is plain markdown followable by ANY capable agent — or by the user manually — because which AI tooling is sanctioned is itself a question the unfold answers. Its own first step (detect) determines what executes the rest. No dependency on any specific harness, language runtime, or feature.
- **Degraded path.** If no AI tooling is allowed on the device at all, the unfold degrades to a manual checklist and the corpus still serves as reading-form conventions. The package is useful at every policy outcome.
- **Re-runnable.** Policy or tooling changes at work → re-run the unfold; it re-resolves the ledger and regenerates `local/`. Idempotent by design.

## Settled principles (founding conversation 2026-08-03, unless dated otherwise)

1. **The seed ships no personal data of any kind.** The corp device is employer-managed and monitored; everything committed is written employer-readable.
2. **Know-how, never infrastructure.** The package ships conventions and procedures, not running systems; the working estate is founded fresh on the device.
3. **One-way flow, upstream → generic → work; no sync channel.** Committed content is employer-generic. Work-derived content stays machine-local (`local/`) and never pushes back. Mirrors the standing IP rule in both directions: nothing from work leaves the employer; nothing private enters this repo.
4. **Scope: portable work-usable conventions + idea/direction notes.** Nothing else.
5. **Build-before-start-date is itself an IP measure:** everything committed predates the maintainer's employment, authored on personal equipment — unambiguously the author's own work.
6. **Installation-package model:** the repo is a self-unfolding seed (section above), never a finished per-employer artifact.
7. **Operating-posture calibration (settled 2026-08-06):** the seed optimizes for sustainable, reliable delivery at minimal cognitive cost, with deliberate relationship investment — not for maximal visible output. Conventions earn their place by reducing the attention a unit of delivered work costs; polish beyond the team's actual bar folds away by default; working-with-the-org practice (expectation alignment, relationship building, visibility) is first-class corpus content, not an afterthought.
8. **Boundary discipline (settled 2026-08-06; reconceived at the 2026-08-17 sitting):** the running work-side installation is self-contained — it runs on the work account, work surfaces, and work budget, and holds day-to-day operations inside the employer boundary; the employment's wider meaning for the user's life is their own affair beyond it. Two binding rules: employer-confidential content stays in employer systems (the IP rule; gray flows decided by work policy, default no) · every record the user keeps on the machine is written at MEANING grain — their own effort-and-value accounting, never employer-confidential material — so whatever they later do with their own records is safe by construction, with no filtering or staging apparatus anywhere. When the user brings a posture or convention update, sessions ingest what they state and never ask for more. Canonical: `corpus/boundary-protocol.md`.
9. **Time-and-presence efficiency (settled 2026-08-09):** the operating posture's second target, alongside principle 7's sustainable-delivery target: deliver the expected impact at minimal total time-and-effort spend. On-site presence is calibrated to the team's REAL practice, never to assumption and never to the written policy alone — intake discovers the actual norm from BOTH the official docs AND direct conversation with teammates (which days people really attend, hours actually kept, how presence is read on the team), then sets presence at the level within that norm that fully serves delivery and relationship investment, not above it by default. In-office time is spent deliberately: the presence-dependent work (1:1s, demos, informal relationship investment) concentrates onto on-site days. Intake items: `knowledge/week1-intake.md` §10.
10. **Team-reality conformance + whole-team collaboration (settled 2026-08-10):** work is not a personal project. A team or org's real conventions may be long-lived, non-standard by outside measures, non-optimal, and documented nowhere — carried in the codebase, its review history, and people's heads. Three consequences. (a) *Discovery:* principle 9's dual-source rule generalizes to ALL conventions — the real practice is discovered from the official docs AND the artifacts (code, merged PRs, review threads) AND teammates directly; where practice diverges from the written guide, the practice IS the convention. (b) *Conformance:* on any team-visible surface the team's real convention wins over the corpus's "optimal" defaults, and non-optimal is not a defect to fix unilaterally — convention change is a social act, proposed through the team's own channels after standing is earned. (c) *Collaboration scope:* the optimization target is the best work RESULT through the best human-agent collaboration, where the humans include the user's colleagues, not just the user — agent-assisted work is written for the teammates who review and maintain it (team idiom, reviewable sizes), the team's actual norms on AI use are discovered at intake and followed, and agent output pace calibrates to what the human review loop can absorb. Intake items: `knowledge/week1-intake.md` §11.
11. **Whole-arc project lifecycle (settled 2026-08-12):** projects are the unit of career-visible work — what is worked on determines delivered impact more than how well it is executed — so the project arc is a FULL standard workflow, never a decision checklist at the accept/refuse moment: continuous sourcing of candidates → active per-candidate intelligence (docs, artifacts, and going to ask people directly — the principle-10 triple source applied to projects) → criteria-based evaluate/decide (alignment · legibility · ownership · growth · dead-end signals · cost · people; outcomes accept / shape / decline-via-priorities / chase / initiate) → communication and negotiation (refusal routed through prioritization, never a bare no; chasing before staffing; the manager as the standing channel) → an initiative lane (own proposals earn their pitch through a cheap time-boxed PoC, through the team's own channel, calibrated to standing) → daily dev under the project frame → periodic sync and presentation riding the team's own ceremonies → landing at a declared end (landed / handed off / killed — all explicit; extension is a conscious re-bet, never a silent lapse). The pipeline runs on a standing work-local portfolio board (unfold-seeded `local/state/projects.md`); selection power is earned — early tenure delivers assigned work reliably while sourcing runs in the background. Module: `corpus/project-lifecycle.md`; intake items: `knowledge/week1-intake.md` §12.
12. **Standing company intelligence (settled 2026-08-12; broadened twice in the same sitting — from direction-only to the general people/events watch, then from org-scoped to company-wide):** what the company's leadership and high-level people are doing is watched continuously, never assumed — what they formally lead, visibly push, and casually say; who is promoted and for what; who departs (exited or laid off) and for what; reorgs, funding, staffing; and the direction it all adds up to. **Scope is the whole company: the own org is watched most closely, but proximity weights attention — it never draws the boundary**; cross-org signals show company direction early and mark people worth binding beyond the silo. Design criterion, same sitting: the seed's systems are designed to the LONG-TERM BEST PRACTICE — smallness of footprint is an acceptable outcome, never the design objective. Structure: a three-level model (company · org · team; alignment is a chain from the work up through the team charter and org priorities to company direction — a chain-break is a first-class finding; the chain is the alignment spine, never the collection boundary) · an evidence ladder grading every signal by durability (resourcing > repetition > planning artifacts > reward evidence > rhetoric; personnel events land at the top grades; the named failure mode is the weathervane who pivots on rhetoric) · one append-only signal log + four registers (unfold-seeded `local/state/company.md`): leaders (formal ownership, what they push, casual signals, trajectory rising/steady/falling), events (promotions/departures/reorgs — ground truth), themes (derived; lifecycle states emerging/rising/established/fading), and the reward map (what the company actually promotes and penalizes — behavior calibration beyond project selection) · RECORD POSTURE (settled 2026-08-12, superseding the same-day device-grain draft): the store lives on the work device and records candidly and completely for the user's benefit — the content is knowledge legitimately gained through ordinary work and violates nothing; completeness is never traded for appearance; the user keeps their own career view beyond the job because it OUTLIVES any single job (lifelong person relationships, career meaning) — the weekly debrief (`corpus/work-record.md`) holds the meaning at record grain; the public lane (exec moves, press coverage, earnings themes) is the user's own concern outside this machine — the work side runs no external feed tooling · collection rides surfaces already attended (all-hands, memos, personnel announcements org-local and company-wide, planning cycles, cross-org channels, the manager 1:1 as the standing direct channel), never a new ceremony · distillation on the team's planning rhythm re-scores the portfolio board's alignment column — the watch is an intelligence organ, never a separate decision channel · action follows the re-aim ladder, cheapest rung first (reframe the narrative → reshape scope → re-bet the portfolio → pre-position skills and relationships, aimed by theme and people trajectory), rungs 2–3 gated on top-grade evidence, the reward map additionally tuning everyday working behavior, standing calibration inherited from principle 11. Module: `corpus/company-watch.md`; intake items: `knowledge/week1-intake.md` §13.
13. **Health floors + the work/personal wall (settled 2026-08-13, amended same sitting):** health ranks above personal life, and both rank far above work — work never buys anything with health. The guard is a FLOOR binding before both delivery targets (principles 7 and 9 optimize strictly inside it): hard floors (sleep · real meals · a hard daily stop · movement/eye cadence · symptoms outrank commitments), enforced — a session that sees a floor being crossed names it at that moment; a floor is crossed only by the user's explicit named exception; breaches are logged and repeated breaches surface in the weekly debrief. Two rulings shape the build. (a) *Grounding:* no concrete practice is invented from assumption — the employer's facility docs are checked FIRST (or the user asked, where docs don't answer) before any instruction binds to a facility, and the guard delivers execute-ready MOMENT INSTRUCTIONS ("already worked an hour → do exactly this"), never principles the user must translate themselves. (b) *Record posture:* the tracker (`local/state/health.md`, daily line + breach log) lives ON the work machine, candid and complete — generalizing the principle-12 record ruling: conservatism about the user's own content on the work device applies only on REAL risk or real company conflict, never by default. The wall isolates work from personal life wherever isolation is possible — no work accounts on personal devices by default, the hard stop, one dedicated home work spot, an evening-protected calendar — with the on-call carve-out (settled same sitting): an on-call rotation's genuinely required channel stays open for its window, scoped to exactly that channel and window — duty, never a breach, logged as on-call events; everything else stays behind the wall. The weekly debrief carries a health-signal line so the user's lifelong health view accumulates (it outlives any job). Module: `corpus/health-guard.md`; intake items: `knowledge/week1-intake.md` §14.

14. **The work record — private truth, rendered public view (settled 2026-08-14):** everything done is recorded, day by day, in ONE private full-grain record on the work machine (`local/state/worklog.md`, unfold-seeded) — activity across and around projects (delivered/progressed work, meetings, help given, ops, learning, candid notes), deliberately NOT the project tracker: the portfolio board holds per-project state, the record cites and never duplicates. Record posture inherits the standing candid-and-complete ruling (principles 12–13). The manager-and-team-visible version is a RENDERED VIEW derived one-way from the private record (private → filtered → public; never two parallel records — the dual-truth ban): delivered work in the team's idiom for its actual readers, the candid grain (friction, time accounting, half-formed judgments) staying private — audience-writing, not concealment. **Where the public view lives is an intake question, never assumed:** the team's existing status conventions (snippets, standup posts, readouts, wiki/status pages, a manager's running 1:1 doc) are discovered docs-and-artifacts-first, then the manager and a teammate asked directly; the discovered convention wins over the module's defaults; until resolved the rendering stages at `local/state/worklog-public.md`. Cadence: private capture daily (sessions append at close; the user adds what only they know in a one-minute end-of-day pass); public render rides the team's rhythm. Consumers: the playbook's manager-alignment running record, the weekly debrief (distilled from it), review-time impact evidence, cold-session memory. Module: `corpus/work-record.md`; intake items: `knowledge/week1-intake.md` §15.

15. **The staged development funnel (settled 2026-08-15):** within an accepted project, execution itself is a structured workflow — decisions settled by cheap evidence, never upfront commitment or debate: a whole-product design pass (through the team's design ritual where one exists; the accept-time premortem aims the earliest experiments at the riskiest assumptions) → substructure design naming components, seams, and the consequential decision points, each with named candidates (the team's incumbent always among them) → per-decision choice tournaments: small time-boxed pre-experiments whose purpose is eliminating the clearly-too-bad cheaply, survivors advancing, every outcome one-lined into the project record → the whole product built as a FEW comparable variants where surviving choices genuinely differ (same seam, same eval harness; variant count set by what the ladder ahead can actually test) → the environment ladder: local test → shared test environment (test AND monitor real telemetry) → the surviving one or two variants to production through the team's release ritual, monitored, previous configuration restorable until the ramp completes → completion DECLARED at the lifecycle's landing bar. Proportionality binds throughout: candidate/variant count and rung formality scale with the cost of being wrong plus the cost of changing later; trivial reversible choices are simply made. Progress reports and presentations are STAGE EVENTS, not afterthoughts — every transition yields a showable artifact (design readout, tournament results, variant comparison, launch readout) riding the lifecycle's sync cadence on the team's own ceremonies; the report is the record filtered for audience, never a parallel narrative. One running project record per active project: the team's doc convention first, else `local/state/projects/<name>.md`. Module: `corpus/dev-workflow.md`; intake items: `knowledge/week1-intake.md` §16.

16. **The work engine — one workflow, agent-run, user-light (settled 2026-08-15):** the corpus's standing disciplines compose into ONE running system — every module a LANE of the whole work workflow — kept running by an **agent-jobs layer** on the work machine, under the work account, inside the employer-sanctioned tooling and its token/compute budget (never any personal account, credential, or token budget — a firm premise of the design). The effort ratio inverts: the user devotes as little attention as possible (typed residue only — authority gate · taste judge · eyes/ground truth · hands, the `human-agent-collaboration.md` vocabulary); the system devotes as much as the budget allows. Mechanics: a job registry (`local/state/agent-jobs.yaml`, unfold-seeded) — per job: lane, cadence, priority, output home, escalation rule, prepare-only gates — run as a **budget-ranked queue** (whatever budget the work account provides burns top-down; priorities/cadences tuned at the Tue/Thu retro against output value vs spend); runtime adaptation by unfold A1 (scheduled agents · session-opening sweep · degraded checklists). Three lanes founded with the engine: **research** (the research workflow ported generically — question → internal-first grounding → hypothesis+eval → experiment loop → honest verdict → readout — plus auto-research jobs: literature/prior-art scouts, eval-landscape watch, experiment monitor, writing assists; `corpus/research-workflow.md`), **connections** (working-relationship investment at every level, distinct from company-watch by aim — register `local/state/people.md`, analysis + prepared moves, the human contact permanently the user's; `corpus/people.md`), **communication craft** (communication as a trained capability harvested from the user's real artifacts — coach passes, one focus area at a time, pre-flight assists; log `local/state/communication.md`; `corpus/communication-craft.md`). **Island calibration (settled same sitting):** the island rule targets the user's own ESTATE (repos, corpus, credentials, agent systems, token budgets); incidental presence on the work machine — a Gmail login, a personal browser profile, ordinary browsing — is normal life and accepted, never engineered around; agent jobs still default to work surfaces only, and anything from beyond the job arrives only in the user's own word. Module: `corpus/work-engine.md`; intake items: `knowledge/week1-intake.md` §17.

17. **The graded boundary + research from the work machine (settled 2026-08-15, second sitting same day):** the boundary is GRADED BY BENEFIT, never absolute — conservatism about the user's own presence on the work machine requires a NAMED concrete risk or real company conflict, never a default (the standing record-posture ruling generalized from records to the whole estate). Four consequences. (a) *Presence:* beyond the incidental presence principle 16 accepted, the user's own TOOLING and RESEARCH may deliberately live on the work machine where they benefit them — the personal secret manager (device-scoped sign-in), an LLM seam library with the user's own keys, clones of their private research repos, and access to their own compute (personally-controlled clusters are the user's resources, never employer resources). (b) *Revocability-first + the footprint register:* everything of the user's enters in its most revocable per-device form and lands a register line at that moment (`local/state/footprint.md`, unfold-seeded); the paired uninstaller `corpus/device-return.md` (return-device prep is a real multi-operation procedure, prepared continuously) walks the register at offboarding, reimage, repair, or loss — issuer-side revocation first, local deletion second, the user's closure note last. (c) *The hard core that remains:* employer-confidential content never exits through the user's own channels (personal API accounts, personal repos, personal compute — the standing IP rule; gray flows decided by the work policy discovered at intake, default no before the check); the user keeps their private life-records off the machine; the work engine keeps running on the work account and budget only (principle 16's premise untouched). (d) *Research sync resolved:* the user's research projects restructure to PRIVATE ENGINE repos in their own estate with public repos as rendered interfaces (the restructure itself is upstream work, outside this package) — the work machine is a CLIENT of that estate, git is the sync, and push feasibility from the device is an intake §18 fact (work docs + peer intelligence), with a read-only-satellite fallback if push is blocked. Modules: `corpus/research-infrastructure.md` + `corpus/device-return.md`; boundary-protocol calibration + credential-rule amendments; intake items: `knowledge/week1-intake.md` §18.

18. **Open-tool voice (settled 2026-08-19; content-scope clause added same day):** the committed repo speaks as an OPEN, user-generic work tool, never as one person's private artifact. The person it serves appears as "the user" ("the maintainer"/"the author" in provenance contexts); decisions carry their settle dates without personal narration; no other estate is named as a counterpart. **Content scope, not just voice: the seed describes only ITSELF** — what the tool is, does, and requires. Knowledge ABOUT the tool held by any particular user's wider estate — what they choose not to bring to it, inventories of what stays out, how the tool relates to their other systems — is THAT USER'S knowledge, homed in their own estate, never written into the seed. Machine-local `local/` content is outside this rule (it is never committed anyway).

19. **The unfold founds an ESTATE of many projects, never one super-repo (settled 2026-08-19, correcting the earlier lanes-in-one-clone target form):** working on the work device is the same kind of working the user does anywhere — an estate of distinct projects, each with its own home, instruction/law layer, task file (`TODO.md`), session board (`NOW.md`), and state — restricted to the work-relevant parts and extended with work-native ones. The corpus modules are GENOMES: each declares the project it seeds. Default roster (module → project): **hub** — the estate's steward-mind: task head, session law, the agent-jobs engine, boundary + footprint, the estate registry, the structure conventions + merged attention view, the council, and the self-model (`task-convention`, `session-discipline`, `work-engine`, `boundary-protocol`, `device-return`, `estate-structure`, `deliberation`, `self-model` — the last three added 2026-08-19/20, principles 20 + 22) · **people** (`connections`) · **company intelligence** (`company-watch`) · **records** (`work-record`, `work-report`) · **health** (`health-guard`) · **communication** (`communication-craft`, `communication-flow` — the flow genome added 2026-08-20, principle 23) · **risk** (`risk-register`) · **resources** (`resources`; row added 2026-08-19, principle 20) · **performance** (`performance`; row added 2026-08-19, principle 20) · **research satellite** (`research-infrastructure`, satellite-registry section; row added 2026-08-19, principle 20 — the user's own research presence on the device, gated on intake §18) · **one project home per accepted work project** (`project-lifecycle`, `dev-workflow`, `research-workflow`) — employer code repos stay employer-owned in employer systems; the estate's per-project homes hold the user's own records and workflow state ABOUT them. Craft/standards modules (`role-playbook`, `engineering-standards`, `agent-reliability`, `human-agent-collaboration`, portable skills) are estate-wide law consumed by every project, not projects themselves. Binding rules: the hub's estate registry maps every project (home, state files) — no unregistered projects; each project is its own directory, a local git repo where device practice allows (backup per A6); corpus references to `local/state/<file>` RESOLVE to the owning project's state home per the registry; the unfold may merge or split roster rows on evidence (A4/A5), reasons recorded in the ledger; the single-clone `local/` form survives only as the degraded fallback where policy or tooling genuinely cannot carry a multi-project estate — never as a convenience default. **The seed is an INSTALLER, never a workplace:** once the estate is founded, the user works in the estate projects — the clone accumulates no living work state (its ledger and log aside) and remains on the device only as the update channel for seed-refresh re-runs (A2); where even that channel goes unused it is deletable without loss.

21. **The autonomy gradient (settled 2026-08-20):** a standing job's trust is graded on three INDEPENDENT axes, deliberately never one fused level (one number cannot carry three different questions; fusing loses the grain that keeps autonomy safe). *Action seriousness* decides the checking each output gets: the irreversibility classes and verification ladder plus the registry's `gate` field, binding from birth. *Capability maturity* decides the auditing cadence: review-everything → review-exceptions → sampled audits; promotions earned by named evidence (a run record at the current stage with zero user corrections) recorded in the entry's `stage` field; demotion free and immediate on any user correction. *Runtime liveness* decides the monitoring, for unattended jobs only: silent death is the failure class, so a scheduled job declares a success marker and a maximum-silence window (`marker` + `silence_h`), checked at every session opening; deploy structure and path consumers register in the entry the day they exist and are consulted before any rename or path change lands. Soft by design: direction only until its two named triggers fire (the first sanctioned scheduled job; the first job trusted past review-everything). Permanent boundaries: real product development is governed by the team's own pipeline, never this gradient (`dev-workflow.md`, team reality first); and maturity buys back the user's review attention, never authority: person-facing, employer-visible, and irreversible steps stay prepare-only at every stage. Module: `corpus/work-engine.md` §The autonomy gradient.

22. **The hub at full steward-mind status: deliberation + the self-model (settled 2026-08-20):** the hub is the estate's complete steward-mind, holding every hub function the maintainer's broader practice proved, at work degree. Two functions added to close the parity. (a) **Deliberation** (`corpus/deliberation.md`): a three-seat council for decision-grade moments — the **first-person seat** (works as the user; grounded in the self-model, the worklog's candid grain, the communication log, the health tracker) · the **positioning seat** (the long-term target; grounded in the promotion dossier, the company store's reward map/themes/trajectories, the people register) · the **delivery seat** (short-term urgency; grounded in the portfolio board, current commitments, the manager's stated expectations). Rules: each seat argues only from its own registers with citations (evidence or silence — seats are groundings, never personas); seats read blind of each other, conflicts stated plainly, never averaged; health floors bind BEFORE deliberation; advisory-only through the standard choice surface, no new decision channel, prepare-only gates untouched; decision records live with the deciding project, estate-wide ones in the hub's `local/state/decisions.md` with an outcome column the periodic self-audit reads (decided-vs-outcome calibration). (b) **The self-model** (`corpus/self-model.md`): the work-grain profile the estate runs on (`local/state/self.md` — identity/title/level/role charter · targets · working style and habits · calibrations incl. the presence baseline and on-site policy facts · the user's standing rules, their wording kept), seeded at unfold from intake + the user's word, maintained by the debrief/retro harvest loop (machine-observed lines labeled; the user's judgment lines change only on their word), work grain only — the user's wider life stays off the machine. **Parity with the maintainer's hub practice is by DEGREE, deliberately partial:** no personified cast (functional seat names — the open-tool voice and the absence of a cast to distinguish), no sovereign-gate execution machinery (prepare-only gates + the user's press carry that weight), no server contracts between estate projects (directories, not services), no feed infrastructure (principle 12), and scope is the JOB, never the user's life. **The name stays `hub`** (examined and kept this sitting): generic, self-describing to any tooling that reads the estate registry, consistent across the seed; a proper name adds nothing where no cast exists.

## The four-axis build model (framing settled 2026-08-04)

The project's content is built along four axes — a provenance layering, each later axis refining or overwriting the earlier (legacy → imagined → observed → lived):

1. **Axis 1 — upstream legacy.** The corpus distilled from the maintainer's established working conventions (v0 landed 2026-08-03). The upstream source is LIVING: the seed re-derives from it, and refresh passes before install are expected rather than exceptional.
2. **Axis 2 — imagined role workflow.** Pre-start, public-source research on the role archetype (applied scientist on an LLM copilot/product team): the likely shape of the work and the workflows/procedures that serve it. Output: a `knowledge/` digest, corpus deltas, and an **assumptions ledger** whose entries become concrete detect questions for the unfold. Provisional by construction — every entry is a guess that axis 3 confirms or refutes at intake.
3. **Axis 3 — intake at start.** The unfold's detect phase plus the first weeks' gathering of internal docs, tooling, and environment on the work device. Work-derived, therefore machine-local (`local/`), never committed.
4. **Axis 4 — lived practice.** Long-term accumulation of real work practices and data on the work device. Machine-local forever.

Binding consequence: **the committed seed evolves only via axes 1–2** (authored upstream, on the maintainer's own equipment — clean provenance). Axes 3–4 never flow back through this repo — the backflow discipline in `ideas.md` applies without exception.
