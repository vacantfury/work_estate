# auto_research — the autonomous research engine

*Work-stack infrastructure (meaning side): the research lane's standing machinery — the jobs that keep research moving with minimal user attention, and the candidate funnel that keeps the next research bets ready (end-state form, user decision 2026-08-24). `research-workflow.md` owns the arc a HUMAN-led research effort walks; this project owns what runs ALONE. It is the platform's first registered workflow family: `autoflow.md` runs it, this project says what it means.*

## The standing jobs (canonical here; moved from `research-workflow.md`)

Registry entries (`work-engine.md`), each writing to its project record or the lane's state, escalating only per its rule:

- **Literature scout** — periodic sweep of the field's new work relevant to active questions (external sources the tooling sanctions; internal research feeds first where they exist); output: a short delta note per active project, not a reading list.
- **Internal prior-art scout** — standing watch on internal docs/experiment records adjacent to active questions; new relevant prior art is a grounding update, sometimes a kill signal — escalate a kill signal.
- **Eval-landscape watch** — what benchmarks/evals/judge practices the field and the org are moving to for the active problem class; feeds the arc's stage 3.
- **Experiment monitor** — running jobs triaged (failed runs, anomalous metrics, done-ness); result lines drafted into the project record; escalate anomalies that block the loop.
- **Analysis and writing assists** — drafting the analysis pass and readout artifacts from the project record for the user's taste-judge pass.

## The candidate funnel

The engine's second half: a ranked, grounded queue of NEXT research candidates, so the portfolio conversation is never improvised.

- **Generate** over the team's live problem space — product gaps, eval failures, company themes (`career.md` themes register), the field's motion — many candidates cheaply.
- **Vet** each survivor: internal prior art (the principle-10 triple source), external literature, eval feasibility, non-overlap with running projects.
- **Package** the few that survive as decision-ready candidates: question, grounding summary, eval-design sketch, expected value, cost shape.

**The gate adaptation (binding):** the funnel never founds a bet. Research selection at a job is negotiated — the funnel's terminal output is a packaged candidate for the user's manager conversation (`project-pipeline.md` sourcing + negotiation doctrine); the user and their manager are the gate. Everything upstream of that conversation is the engine's work; the conversation itself is the user's.

## Founding gate

The standing jobs found with the engine's registry (they are unfold-seeded entries). The funnel founds when the user first wants a next-bet queue — typically at the first "what should I work on next" conversation with the manager in view.
