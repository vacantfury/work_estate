# Self-model — the work-grain profile the estate runs on

*The one grounding file about the user that every session and the council's first-person seat read (design principle 22). An estate personalizes to its user or it stays generic tooling; this file is where the personalization lives. Work grain only (`boundary-protocol.md`): who the user is AT WORK, what they aim for there, and how they work best. The user's wider life stays their own affair beyond this machine.*

## The state file — `local/state/self.md` (hub)

- **Identity** — title, level, team, manager, and the role charter as stated at intake (§8–§9): what the role is expected to own and deliver.
- **Targets** — delivery targets as agreed with the manager; the growth/promotion target (one headline line, citing the performance dossier rather than duplicating it); the user's own stated priorities, in their words.
- **Working style** — stated and observed habits: the hours the user works best, focus patterns, meeting and communication preferences, AI-collaboration preferences (what they delegate, what they keep in their own hands), strengths and growth edges at work.
- **Calibrations** — the presence baseline and hours norms (intake §10 output), on-site policy facts as they bind this user, sustainable-pace facts, on-call posture.
- **Standing rules** — the user's own orders to the estate ("always X", "never Y"), dated, their wording kept.

## Maintenance

- **Seeded at unfold** from the intake answers plus what the user states; grows by dated, append-preferring lines.
- **The harvest loop:** the weekly debrief and the twice-weekly retro may PROPOSE self-model updates from observed practice (e.g. "the record shows deep work lands before noon"). Machine-observed lines are labeled as such; the user's own judgment lines change only on the user's word (the data-shape rule's two layers, `estate-structure.md`).
- **Staleness is a defect:** a session that learns the title, team, manager, targets, or a calibration changed updates the file the same session.

## Consumers

Session personalization (tone, pacing, what to surface and what to leave silent) · the council's first-person seat (`deliberation.md` — this file is that seat's spine) · `communication-craft.md` (the writer's voice) · `health-guard.md` (calibration) · `work-report.md` (review context). A session acting against the self-model's standing rules is wrong even when its output is good.

## Fold-away note (for the unfold's A5 pass)

Core-adopt at every policy outcome: one file at meaning grain, candid and complete, kept for the user's benefit.
