# Career — advancement in this company

*The advancement project (design principles 20a + 26). SCOPE: this company only — promotion, review, growth, positioning, including outside-facing work done AS this role (collaborations, talks); moving to another company is out of scope and off this estate. The employer's review instrument is where delivered work converts into rating, compensation, and level (the resulting pay components and their windows are `compensation.md`'s register); the promotion case is a multi-cycle argument assembled from evidence. Both are READS of records the estate already keeps: the worklog (`work-record.md`), the portfolio board (`project-lifecycle.md`), and the reward map (`company-watch.md`). This project makes those reads standing instead of reconstructed: review text written from memory at cycle time is exactly the evidence loss the daily record exists to prevent.*

## The two artifacts

- **Review readiness — `local/state/review.md`.** A continuously-maintained draft in the shape of the employer's own review instrument (discovered at intake §20: the instrument, its cadence, its rubric, and the calibration process as actually practiced): impact claims each anchored to dated worklog lines and project records, collaboration and contribution evidence, the period's story. At any moment the draft is current; writing the real submission is an edit, never an archaeology dig.
- **The promotion case — `local/state/promotion.md`.** A standing dossier against the NEXT level's bar, discovered rather than assumed: the leveling guide as written, plus how promotions actually happen here (the company watch's events register shows who was promoted and for what). Contents: the bar's dimensions · current evidence per dimension · the named gaps · which candidate projects would close them. The gap list feeds the project pipeline's selection criteria (`project-lifecycle.md`); the reward map calibrates which evidence actually counts.

## Growth — development investments

Career-development opportunities, moved here from the claims register (split 2026-08-24): programs (patent awards, internal mobility, mentorship), internal speaking and teaching, hackathons, trainings, and growth EVENTS — each candidate event gets one attendance read per the data-shape rule (learning · visibility · people, against time cost; person-aims read with the people register in view) ending in an attend/skip recommendation; the user decides. State: `local/state/growth.md`, windows landing as dated due lines like any claim.

## Positioning — what this company rewards

The theme/reward register (meaning home of the company watch, `company-watch.md` — the watch runs as a standing capture job, its signals typed into the store): direction themes with lifecycle and evidence grade, the reward map (who is promoted and for what), portfolio implications. State: `local/state/themes.md`. Person-signals route to the people cards, never here.

## Standing jobs (registry entries, `work-engine.md`)

- **Evidence harvest** — periodic pass over the worklog and project records, appending review-grade evidence lines (dated, specific, impact-stated) to the review draft.
- **Case refresh** — on the team's planning rhythm: re-read the promotion dossier against new evidence and reward-map movement; refresh the gap list; flag any gap that has sat unaddressed across cycles.
- **Cycle preparation** — when a review cycle opens: the submission package drafted from the standing artifacts in the instrument's shape, plus a self-calibration read (where the record is strong or thin against the rubric). The submission is the user's press; review and promotion conversations are the user's voice, prepared and never delegated.

## Rules

1. **Evidence over narrative.** Every claim cites dated record lines; a claim with no citation is a gap to fill, not a sentence to polish.
2. **The rubric as practiced wins over the rubric as written** (design principle 10). Calibrate against what the reward map shows the company actually promoting and penalizing, not against the guide's prose alone.
3. **Record posture: candid and complete.** The dossier states real gaps plainly; a flattering dossier is useless to its only reader.
