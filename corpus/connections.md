# Connections — the working-relationship lane

*Deliberate investment in working relationships at every level (design principle 16). Distinct from `company-watch.md` by aim: the watch is intelligence ABOUT leadership and direction; this lane is investment IN relationships — teammates, the manager, cross-team collaborators, org peers, and the high-level people worth knowing. The two feed each other: the watch's people trajectories aim this lane's investment; this lane's contacts feed the watch's signal log.*

## The register — `local/state/people.md`

One entry per person who matters to the work, appended as they appear (meetings, reviews, threads — surfaces already attended, never scraped):

- **Who** — name, role, what they actually own or drive (not the org-chart line).
- **What they care about** — observed, from what they push, ask, and praise.
- **Interaction log** — append-only one-liners: date, context, substance, anything owed or promised either way.
- **Relationship state** — coarse: new / working / invested / dormant; plus the one-line "what this relationship is for" once it's clear.
- **Next move** — the current smallest sensible investment (a question to ask, help to offer, a follow-up owed), or none.

Record posture: candid and complete, kept for the user's benefit (standing ruling, principles 12–14) — honest reads of people are what make the register useful; completeness is never traded for appearance.

## Standing jobs (registry entries, `work-engine.md`)

- **Register upkeep** — harvest the worklog and meeting notes for new people and new interactions; append entries and log lines; flag entries gone stale.
- **Relationship analysis** — periodic pass ranking where investment is due: who is owed a follow-up, which relationships the active projects and rising themes (company-watch) make newly valuable, which invested relationships are going dormant; output: refreshed next-move lines.
- **Move preparation** — for each due next-move, the prepared package (the question drafted, the context recalled, the help scoped). **The human contact itself is permanently the user's** — every job here is prepare-only; warmth cannot be delegated and never appears to be.

## Rules of the lane

1. **Investment rides real work.** The default move is being genuinely useful inside actual collaboration — help given, good reviews, credit shared — never networking theater. The playbook's relationship-investment posture (`role-playbook.md`) governs tone and pace.
2. **Sources are ordinary surfaces.** Meetings, code review, channels, all-hands. No collection beyond what ordinary presence provides.
3. **The job serves the work; the lifelong layer lives beyond it.** Relationships that outlive the job are the user's own affair beyond this installation — the weekly debrief's people line (`work-record.md`) holds the meaning at record grain; the work register never syncs anywhere and is never copied.
