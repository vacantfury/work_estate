# Connections — the working-relationship system

*Deliberate investment in working relationships at every level (design principle 16; upgraded to relationship-system grade 2026-08-19, design principle 20b). Distinct from `company-watch.md` by aim: the watch is intelligence ABOUT leadership and direction; this system is investment IN relationships — teammates, the manager, cross-team collaborators, org peers, and the high-level people worth knowing. The two feed each other: the watch's people trajectories aim this system's investment; this system's contacts feed the watch's signal log.*

## The register — one card per person, `local/state/people.md`

One CARD per person who matters to the work, appended as they appear (meetings, reviews, threads — surfaces already attended, never scraped):

- **Who** — name, role, what they actually own or drive (not the org-chart line).
- **What they care about** — observed, from what they push, ask, and praise.
- **Interaction log** — append-only one-liners: date, context, substance, anything owed or promised either way.
- **Threads** — the card's live waits (next section).
- **Relationship state** — coarse: new / working / invested / dormant; plus the one-line "what this relationship is for" once it's clear; plus a natural cadence once one exists (e.g. weekly 1:1, monthly coffee).
- **Next move** — the current smallest sensible investment (a question to ask, help to offer, a follow-up owed), or none.

**Two layers, kept separate (the data-shape rule, `estate-structure.md`):** observed facts and the system's reads are labeled as such; the user's own judgment lines are theirs, dated, and never overwritten. Corrections append; nothing is edited away.

Record posture: candid and complete, kept for the user's benefit (standing ruling, principles 12–14) — honest reads of people are what make the register useful; completeness is never traded for appearance.

## Threads and the due lane — the system notices silence

Every live exchange that awaits someone is a **thread** on the card: a question asked, help promised, an intro pending, a reply owed in either direction.

```
thread: <what it is about> · last: YYYY-MM-DD in|out · next: <who owes what>
```

The due pass reads all threads and cadences and surfaces, ranked:

- replies the **user owes** (always first);
- threads gone **silent past their natural rhythm** (a nudge may be due, or a graceful close);
- **cadence lapses** — invested relationships drifting toward dormant, 1:1s and recurring touchpoints slipping;
- **newly-valuable investments** — people the active projects, the promotion case's gaps, and the watch's rising themes make worth knowing now.

The point of the lane: **the user never tracks who they owe or who has gone quiet — the system does.** A wait that lives only in the user's head is a defect of the register, fixed by carding it.

## Campaigns — deliberate pushes

Investment larger than one next-move (meeting the whole org in the first quarter, building sponsorship toward the promotion case, getting to know a partner team before a collaboration) runs as a campaign per `estate-structure.md`: a named end, a small roster of cards, moves surfacing through the ordinary due lane, a decided close. The campaign form keeps pushes finite; relationships themselves never "complete."

## Standing jobs (registry entries, `work-engine.md`)

- **Register upkeep** — harvest the worklog and meeting notes for new people, new interactions, and new or resolved threads; append cards and log lines; flag entries gone stale.
- **Due-lane pass** — the thread/cadence computation above, refreshed on the engine's rhythm; output: the ranked due list and refreshed next-move lines.
- **Move preparation** — for each due move, the prepared package (the question drafted, the context recalled, the help scoped). **The human contact itself is permanently the user's** — every job here is prepare-only; warmth cannot be delegated and never appears to be.

## Rules of the system

1. **Investment rides real work.** The default move is being genuinely useful inside actual collaboration — help given, good reviews, credit shared — never networking theater. The playbook's relationship-investment posture (`role-playbook.md`) governs tone and pace.
2. **Sources are ordinary surfaces.** Meetings, code review, channels, all-hands. No collection beyond what ordinary presence provides.
3. **The job serves the work; the lifelong layer lives beyond it.** Relationships that outlive the job are the user's own affair beyond this installation — the weekly debrief's people line (`work-record.md`) holds the meaning at record grain; the work register never syncs anywhere and is never copied.

The register rides the store service (`store.md`); the harvest and the due lane's silence checks query channels through the search bus (`search.md`).
